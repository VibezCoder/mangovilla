Chapter I · Roots

Dr. Amit & Dr. Neeti Salvi
Founders · Nachane, Ratnagiri

01

A home first, a homestay second.

The Mango Villa began with a husband-and-wife dream. Dr. Amit and Dr. Neeti Salvi — two doctors with a keen interest in hospitality — imagined travellers choosing a well-kept home over a glitzy hotel. So they built one.

More than a decade later, that idea has matured into a grove. Laterite walls, red-tiled verandas and mango trees kept with the same care as the rooms beneath them. Large sit-outs, generous attached bathrooms, and quiet corners for doing absolutely nothing.

The comforts are modern where they matter — air conditioning, LED televisions, intercoms and room service — and the top-end rooms add jacuzzi bathtubs and walk-in closets. Around it all, the property remains a source of income for local families.
